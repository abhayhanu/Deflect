# Refund Timelines

This policy explains when a refund is issued and how long it takes to reach the customer. It does not decide whether a refund is owed. That is decided by the policy for the situation.

## When the refund is issued

- **Lost, damaged or not as described claims:** as soon as the claim is approved.
- **Cancellations:** at the moment the order is cancelled.
- **Returns:** within 2 business days after the item is picked up and passes inspection.

## How long it takes to arrive

Counted in business days from the day the refund is issued.

| Payment method | Time to reach the customer |
| --- | --- |
| Wallet | Within 24 hours |
| UPI | 1 to 3 business days |
| Net banking | 3 to 5 business days |
| Credit or debit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Cash on delivery | 5 to 7 business days by NEFT, after bank details are shared |

## Cash on delivery orders

- Refunds for cash on delivery orders are paid by NEFT to the customer's bank account.
- We email the customer a secure link to share their bank details. The refund is sent within 5 to 7 business days after the details are submitted.
- Never ask for or accept bank account numbers, card numbers, CVV, OTP or UPI PIN in a support conversation.

## Refund not received

- If the refund is still within the time for its payment method, share the date it was issued and the date it should arrive by.
- If the refund has not arrived more than **2 business days after the latest expected date**, share the refund reference and pass it to the support team, who will trace it with the bank.

## Limits

- The total refunded on an order can never exceed the amount paid for it.
- Once a refund is issued, it cannot be reversed or sent to a different account or payment method.
