# Damaged on Arrival

This policy covers items that arrived physically damaged: broken, cracked, crushed, dented, torn or leaking. It does not cover the wrong item or a missing item (see `pol_not_as_described`), or an item that stopped working after normal use (manufacturer warranty).

## Reporting window

- Transit damage must be reported within **72 hours** of the delivery scan.

## Reported within 72 hours

- **Item price up to Rs 10,000 (inclusive):** refund the price of the damaged item with reason code `damaged`. The customer does not need to send it back and should dispose of it safely.
- **Item price above Rs 10,000:** the item must be picked up and inspected before any refund. The support team arranges the inspection pickup. Do not issue a refund.
- If only one item in a multi item order is damaged, refund only that item's price.
- If the customer prefers to send the item back instead, a free return pickup can be created. The refund is then issued after pickup, as described in `pol_return_window`.

## Reported after 72 hours

- Damage reported after 72 hours is no longer treated as transit damage and cannot be refunded straight away.
- If the report is within 7 days of delivery, offer a free return pickup. The refund is issued after the item is picked up and passes inspection.
- After 7 days, the item is covered only by the manufacturer warranty, where one exists.

## Safety

- If the damage involves a swollen or leaking battery, smoke, sparks, a burning smell or any injury, stop and follow `pol_escalation`. Do not process a refund or a return.
