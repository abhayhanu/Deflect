# Item Not as Described

This policy covers an order that arrived but does not match what the customer bought:

- the wrong product
- the wrong size, colour or variant compared to the order (not a size the customer chose and now dislikes, which is a normal return under `pol_return_window`)
- a product that is materially different from its listing, such as a different model or missing listed features
- an item missing from a multi item package

Packages that never arrived are covered by `pol_lost_transit`. Physical damage is covered by `pol_damaged_goods`.

## Reporting window

- Claims must be raised within **7 days** of the delivery scan. After 7 days, the claim is not eligible under this policy.

## Resolution

- **Item price up to Rs 7,500 (inclusive):** refund the price of the affected item with reason code `not_as_described`. The customer does not need to return it.
- **Item price above Rs 7,500:** the item must be picked up and verified before any refund. The support team arranges this. Do not issue a refund.
- **Missing item:** refund the listed price of the missing item, under the same Rs 7,500 limit.
- If the customer prefers to send the item back, a free return pickup can be created instead, and the refund follows `pol_return_window`.

## Counterfeit claims

- If the customer says the product is fake or counterfeit, do not refund and do not argue. The support team verifies these with the brand and replies within 3 business days.
