# Changing the Delivery Address

This policy covers requests to change where an order will be delivered, including the flat number, street, landmark, pin code or the delivery contact number.

## When the address can be changed

- Only while the order status is **placed**, meaning it has not shipped yet.
- Only by the customer who placed the order.
- The new address must be in the **same city** as the current delivery address.

## When it cannot be changed

- **Order already shipped:** the address is locked with the courier and cannot be changed. The customer can ask someone at the address to receive it, or refuse the delivery. A refused order returns to our warehouse and is refunded in full within 7 days of the refusal.
- **Different city:** a move to another city is not possible on an existing order. If the order has not shipped, the customer can cancel it (`pol_cancellation`) and place a new order with the new address.

## High value orders

- For orders with a total above **Rs 25,000**, an address change needs identity verification by the support team. Do not change the address.

## What to confirm

- Repeat the new address back to the customer and confirm that the promised delivery date does not change for a change within the same city.
