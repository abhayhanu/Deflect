# Returns and Return Window

This policy covers customers who want to send back an item they received because they changed their mind, it does not fit, or they do not like it. Damaged items follow `pol_damaged_goods` and wrong or missing items follow `pol_not_as_described`.

## Return window

- A return must be requested within **7 days (168 hours)** of the delivery scan. After that the window is closed and the return cannot be accepted.
- Only delivered orders can be returned. An order that has not been delivered yet follows `pol_cancellation`.
- The item must be unused, with its original packaging, tags, accessories and any free gifts.
- One return request per order.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Innerwear, lingerie and socks
- Personal care and grooming products once the seal is opened
- In ear earphones and earbuds (wired or wireless), for hygiene reasons. Over ear and on ear headphones can be returned.
- Gift cards
- Grocery and other perishables
- Customised or personalised products
- Software and digital downloads

## Phones, laptops and tablets

- Mobile phones, laptops and tablets cannot be returned for change of mind or because the customer is unhappy with performance.
- They are still covered for transit damage (`pol_damaged_goods`) and for the wrong item (`pol_not_as_described`).
- Faults after delivery are handled by the brand's authorised service centre under the manufacturer warranty.

## Fees

- **Change of mind returns:** a reverse pickup fee of Rs 99 is deducted from the refund.
- Returns for damage or the wrong item are free.

## How a return works

1. We create a return pickup. The courier collects the item within 2 business days.
2. The item is inspected within 2 business days of pickup.
3. If it passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method. Timelines are in `pol_refund_timelines`.

## Exchanges

- We do not offer direct exchanges. The customer can return the item and place a new order for the one they want.
